Here is a recipe for Torta Caprese

Ingredients

  • 125g almond flour
  • 125g dark chocolate
  • 125g unsalted butter
  • 125g sugar
  • 3 large eggs, separated

Directions

  • Preheat oven to 320F
  • Melt chocolate and butter in a bowl, add sugar and almond flour to it
  • Once cool, add egg yolks and stir
  • In a separate bowl whip egg whites to soft peaks
  • Fold egg whites into chocolate mixture
  • Pour into pan and bake 40 minutes
  • Let cool completely and eat
